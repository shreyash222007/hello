Biography

A life lived in service to the nation

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a humble family. Despite modest means, his insatiable curiosity and relentless dedication propelled him to become one of India's most celebrated scientists and statesmen.

He joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) in 1958 and later moved to the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), where he led the development of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle — SLV-III. His pivotal role in the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme earned him the moniker "Missile Man of India."

As Chief Scientific Adviser to the Government and Secretary of DRDO, Dr. Kalam was instrumental in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 1998, elevating India's stature as a nuclear power on the world stage. Elected the 11th President of India in 2002, he became known as the "People's President" for his accessibility, humility, and unwavering belief in India's youth.

He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture he loved — inspiring students — at IIM Shillong. His legacy endures as a beacon of scientific temper, courage, and an unshakeable dream for a developed India.

Milestones

Extraordinary Achievements

🛰️

SLV-III Success

Led the team that built India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle, successfully deploying the Rohini satellite in 1980.

🚀

IGMDP Architect

Directed the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, producing Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Trishul and Nag missiles.

⚛️

Pokhran-II

Coordinated the 1998 nuclear tests that established India as a de-facto nuclear weapons state and transformed national defence.

🏛️

11th President

Served as President of India from 2002 to 2007, inspiring millions with his vision of "India 2020" as a developed nation.

🏅

Bharat Ratna

Awarded India's highest civilian honour in 1997, along with Padma Vibhushan (1990) and Padma Bhushan (1981).

📖

Author & Teacher

Wrote landmark books including Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020, inspiring generations of students and leaders.

Journey

A Timeline of Greatness



1931

Born in Rameswaram

Born to Jainulabdeen and Ashiamma in a small Tamil Nadu fishing town. Distributed newspapers as a child to fund his studies.

1958

Joins DRDO

Began his defence research career, working on developing a small hovercraft for the Indian Army.

1969

Transfers to ISRO

Moved to the Indian Space Research Organisation and spearheaded India's first SLV mission as Project Director.

1980

SLV-III Success

India's first indigenously built satellite launch vehicle placed Rohini satellite in orbit — a landmark for the nation.

1992–99

Chief Scientific Adviser

Served as Principal Scientific Adviser to the Government of India and Secretary, DRDO.

1998

Pokhran-II Tests

Led Operation Shakti — five nuclear tests that demonstrated India's full thermonuclear capability.

2002

Elected President

Became the 11th President of India with overwhelming cross-party support — a scientist ascending to the highest office.

2015

Eternal Rest at IIM Shillong

Passed away during a lecture titled "A Liveable Planet Earth" — doing what he loved most, inspiring young minds.
